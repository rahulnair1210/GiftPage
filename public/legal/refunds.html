Refunds and cancellations

Last updated

This is a starting template, not legal advice. Fill in your details in /assets/site.js and have it reviewed before you go live.

Building and previewing a gift is free. You only pay when you publish, and you can see exactly what the recipient will see before you do.

When we refund

  • You were charged but your gift didn't publish, and we can't fix it.
  • You were charged more than once for the same gift.
  • Your gift doesn't work because of a fault on our side that we can't fix within 48 hours.

When we don't

Because the gift is digital and delivered immediately, we don't refund once it's published and working, including if you change your mind or the recipient doesn't open it. You can always keep editing it instead.

How to ask

Email within 7 days of payment with your gift link and payment reference. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
