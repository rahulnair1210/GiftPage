This policy explains what , which runs Giftpage, collects and why.

What we collect

  • Gift content: the names, text, dates and photos you put in a gift.
  • Your contact details: your email address and, if you give it, your phone number, collected when you publish.
  • Payment details: handled by Razorpay. We receive a payment reference and status, never your card or UPI details.
  • Reports: if someone reports a gift, the reason and details they give.

How we use it

Only to build, store and show your gift, to send you your links and receipts, to process payments, and to handle reports. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who can see a gift

Anyone with its link. Gift pages are hidden from search engines. Photos and messages are not sent to a visitor's device until the gift's opening time.

Services we use

Supabase (database and photo storage), Vercel (hosting), Razorpay (payments) and Resend (email). They process data on our behalf.

How long we keep it

  • Unpublished drafts: deleted after 30 days without changes.
  • Published gifts: deleted one year after the opening date.
  • You can delete a gift and all its photos at any time from your edit link.
