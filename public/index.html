Questions

Can they open it before their birthday?

No. Until 12:00 AM on the date you pick, our server only sends the countdown. The photos and messages aren't even downloaded to their phone until the doors open, so changing the phone's clock doesn't help.

Do they need to install anything?

No. It's a normal web link that opens in any browser, on phone or computer.

Can I change things after I've paid?

Yes. Use the private edit link we email you. Changes go live straight away, and you can even move the opening date.

Who can see it?

Only people with the link. Gift pages are hidden from search engines, and you can delete a gift and all its photos at any time.

How long does it stay online?

For a year after it opens. After that, the gift and its photos are deleted automatically.

What if something goes wrong with my payment?

If money was taken but your gift didn't publish, it's usually matched automatically within a few minutes. If not, email us and we'll sort it out or refund you. See the refund policy.
